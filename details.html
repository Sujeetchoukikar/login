Application Guide

Step 1 of 5
Personal Details
  • Your name and DOB are pre-filled from your registration — they cannot be edited.
  • Select your gender from the dropdown carefully.
  • Enter your parents' full legal names exactly as in official documents.
  • Choose the correct reservation category — it affects your eligibility.
Step 2 of 5
Contact & Address
  • Use a valid, active email address — communication will be sent here.
  • Your phone number is locked to your registered mobile.
  • Enter your complete current residential address.
  • Pincode must be a valid 6-digit Indian postal code.
Step 3 of 5
ID & Academics
  • PAN format: 5 letters + 4 digits + 1 letter (e.g. ABCDE1234F).
  • Aadhaar must be exactly 12 digits — no spaces or dashes.
  • College field appears only for Diploma, UG, and PG selections.
  • Double-check all ID numbers before proceeding.
Step 4 of 5
Upload Documents
  • Each file must be under 1MB — compress if needed.
  • Photo should be a recent passport-size image (JPG/PNG).
  • Signature must be on a plain white background.
  • Aadhaar and PAN uploads must be clearly legible scans.
  • Category certificate is optional but speeds up verification.
Step 5 of 5
Payment
  • Application fee is a one-time, non-refundable ₹500.
  • Supported: UPI, Net Banking, Cards via Razorpay.
  • Do not close the window while payment is processing.
  • Download your PDF receipt after successful payment.

⚠ Important: Review each step carefully before clicking Next. You cannot go back and edit after payment is submitted. Keep your PDF receipt as proof of submission.
